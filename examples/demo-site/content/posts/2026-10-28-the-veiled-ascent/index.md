Every member walks the same path. The ranks are not honours; they are descriptions of where you are.

| Rank | You have arrived when | The rite |
|---|---|---|
| I. The Seeker | You notice the brokenness and decide to act, however small the act. | Mend one thing. |
| II. The Hand | You have mended for others without being asked, and without being thanked. | Mend in secret for a season. |
| III. The Architect | You teach others to see what is broken and how it might be made whole. | Lead a mending. |

---

Some rules are short enough for a small table:

| Day | Mission |
|---|---|
| Monday | Fix one object |
| Tuesday | Call one person |

The ascent is veiled because you cannot see the next step until you have taken this one.
