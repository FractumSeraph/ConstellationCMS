Each rite has the same shape. The steps are numbered so they can be followed under pressure.

### The First Rite

#### 1. The Approach

Find the broken thing. Do not announce yourself.

#### 2. The Work

Mend it with what you have. If you have nothing, your attention is enough.

#### 3. The Departure

Leave before thanks can be given.

### Notes for keepers

1. Keep a ledger of what was mended.
2. Never record who mended it.
   - Not even in private notes.
   - Not even to yourself.
3. Read the ledger aloud at the solstice.

```text
Ledger, entry 41
  mended: a gate hinge, 3rd street
  by: —
```

[^1]: The ledger exists so that the work is remembered, not the worker.
