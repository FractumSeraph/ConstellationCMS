**We stand at a stark truth: the world is fundamentally broken.**

Look around. The evidence of the *Fractum*, the great brokenness, is undeniable. It is in the widening chasm between wealth and poverty, and in the pervasive isolation of the modern soul. This brokenness is not a romantic flaw; it is a **crisis demanding action**.

## The Vow

> Mend what you can.<br>
> Empathy before judgement.<br>
> Leave every place a little less broken than you found it.

## What we are not

- We are not a charity, though we give.
- We are not a church, though we gather.
- We are not finished, and we never will be.
