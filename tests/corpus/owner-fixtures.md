**We stand at a stark truth: the world is fundamentally broken.**

Look around. The evidence of the *Fractum*, the great brokenness, is undeniable.

## The Vow

> Mend what you can.<br>
> Empathy before judgement.<br>
> Leave every place a little less broken than you found it.

### The First Rite

#### 1. The Approach

Find the broken thing.

#### 2. The Work

Mend it with what you have.

| Rank | You have arrived when | The rite |
|---|---|---|
| I. The Seeker | You notice the brokenness and decide to act, however small the act. | Mend one thing. |
